Who I am

About Me

Hi, I'm Devika — a yoga instructor who believes movement should feel like play, not punishment. My classes are warm, encouraging, and always a little fun.

I blend mindful breathwork with dynamic sequencing, creating sessions that leave you energised, centred, and smiling. Whether you're stepping on the mat for the first time or deepening an existing practice, you'll always feel welcome here.

Beyond the mat, I'm passionate about holistic wellness — weaving in philosophy, pranayama, and a good dose of laughter into every class.
